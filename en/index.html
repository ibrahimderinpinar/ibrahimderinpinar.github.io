About

İbrahim Nuri Derinpınar practices in corporate law, contract law, real estate law, lease law, sports law, esports law, transport law, arbitration. His work focuses on securing commercial relationships, managing contractual processes efficiently, and preventing disputes for domestic and international clients.
Following his graduation from Istanbul University Faculty of Law, he earned his LL.M. with distinction at Brunel University of London and participated in commercial law and competition law studies at the London School of Economics and Political Science. His doctoral work has focused especially on contract law, sports law, and esports law.

Academic Studies

Ph.D. Dissertation

Bahçeşehir University Private Law Doctoral Program

The Esports Player Contract

This thesis examines the legal framework for esports player agreements in Turkish law from a comparative perspective, focusing on the parties’ rights and obligations, validity requirements, and termination regime.

Master’s Thesis

Brunel University of London

Continuing Legal Uncertainty and Complexity in the Field of International Multimodal Transport Liability

It assesses legal uncertainty in the carrier’s liability regime for international multimodal transport and the practical impact of different liability systems.
